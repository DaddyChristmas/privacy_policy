Privacy Policy for Sunblade Shores

Last updated: September 2026

Sunblade Shores is an offline single-player helicopter simulation game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sunblade Shores does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics:

  • No user accounts or sign-ins are required.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs are included.
  • No network, location, contacts, camera, or sensor data is transmitted off your device.

2. Local Save Data

All gameplay progression, unlocked aircraft, and settings are stored strictly locally in your device's private app sandbox. This data is never sent to external servers and is removed when you uninstall the game.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the app collects zero personal data, it fully complies with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.

4. Contact
